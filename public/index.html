    Adding members

    Use Add a member. The form has four short steps (personal, campus, fellowship, family) and a final review. Fields marked * are required.

    Finding members

    Open Members, then search by name, course or phone. Press ⌘ K (or Ctrl K) to jump to search.

    Viewing, editing and removing

    Click a member to open their profile. Use Edit details to update the record or Remove to delete it. If someone else saved changes while you were editing, you’ll be asked to reload first.

    Reports

    On Reports, download the member directory or the monthly overview as CSV files.

    Accounts and access

    Campus pastors see only their own centre. The regional admin manages accounts on Users and can reset forgotten passwords. You’re signed out after 30 minutes without activity.

    Audit log

    Every sign-in, view, change, export and denied access attempt is recorded. The regional admin can review it on Audit log and use Verify integrity to confirm nothing has been altered.
